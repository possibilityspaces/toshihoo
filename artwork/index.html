ARTWORK

At the heart of all of my work is a passion for creativity and the drive to explore not only what is possible, but what is meaningful and beautiful.

My art projects are diverse in medium and style, but they all share a common theme of my love of light.

SELECTED ART PROJECTS

THE EVOLUTION OF FIRE

Commissioned artwork and event production. Stop-motion animation light painting utilizing custom fiber optic brushes. The animated short explores the theme of human's long and evolving relationship with fire, from ancient ritual to modern technologies.

ROLE: Artist/Producer COLLABORATORS: Eric Freeman, & Carl Bridge MORE INFO: Project Website

Passages: Moving Through Worlds

A collection of photographic prints captured in Turkey, Israel, Brazil, France and the USA.

This collection has been exhibited at a number of galleries including The Wall Gallery (Oakland, CA), 550 Bryant (San Francisco, CA), and Cloud9 (Berkeley, CA).

My photography is represented by the SFMOMA Artist Gallery

ROLE: Photographer

BEARDYMAN: COHERENCE 2

Video excerpt from 3 days of improvised music sessions in my studio/lab with famed UK composer and beatboxer, Beardyman.

ROLE: Artist/Producer COLLABORATORS: Beardyman, Daniel Berkman, Moldover, RichDDT, Liza Bender

WATER DOUBLE

Video art installation of slo-motion water footage. Shot with Sony FS700 and formated for square display system

ROLE: Artist/Producer MUSIC: Honey Circuit COLLABORATORS: Eric Freeman and Liza Bender

DISCO CITY TIMELAPSE

HDR timelapse of the San Francisco Bay from the Marin Headlands.

ROLE: Photographer

Obey TV

One of my earliest video creations. A deconstruction of CRT's and an homage to Nam June Paik and the Emergency Broadcast Network. I still love to watch this piece on days when I've been battling with technology.

ROLE: Director/Editor/TV Collector
