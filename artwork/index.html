The Resolution of Water

An interactive light-refraction sculpture of distilled water, raw wood, reclaimed thermoplastic, a silicone lens and a suspended pendant light. A visitor's touch disrupts the water, casting chaotic light patterns that slowly resolve back to equilibrium — the temporal labor of environmental recovery made visible.
