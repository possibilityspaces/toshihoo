Research & experiential prototyping

As Director of the Emerging Media Lab at the Institute for the Future (2016–2026), Toshi led a prototyping studio and independent research group examining the evolving relationship between humans and sophisticated media systems.

IFTF Emerging Media Lab · 2018

Social VR: Leading Edge Behaviors

Co-directed (with Lyn Jeffery and Liza Bender) an ethnographic study identifying ten leading behaviors in early 3D social worlds — analyzing how digital embodiment impacts retail, work and communication.

IFTF Emerging Media Lab · with Dylan Hendricks

Simtainer

Co-created an interactive virtual reality platform for experiential storytelling, built with an immersive VR engine and custom projection mapping — letting users feel the visceral second-order consequences of systemic future shifts.

IFTF Emerging Media Lab · Creative Director

Four Daniels

A volumetric filmmaking experiment rendering a four-part musical performance by composer Daniel Berkman as an interactive 3D point-cloud — exploring the transition from flat screens to spatial media. Built with DepthKit, Kinect, Three.js and Unity, with technical direction by Akmyrat Tuyliyev and production design by Liza Bender.

MIT Media Lab hackathon · Producer

Elpis

A target-based AR application created in 24 hours, projecting a 3D volumetric live performance by musician Christen Lien into physical space using DepthKit volumetric capture, Unity and Vuforia.

IFTF × NTT Data · Co-author

Three Horizons of AI-Enabled Work

A major foresight report analyzing how AI will shift from passive tool to active thought partner and "versatilist" collaborator — co-authored with Jorge Camacho, Ben Hamamoto, Rod Falcon and Susanne Forcheimer.
