Production

Immersive experiences, live events and film — produced with interdisciplinary teams of artists, engineers and performers.

Feature film · Co-director / Co-producer · 2007–2009

The Singularity Is Near: A True Story About the Future

A hybrid documentary and science-fiction feature film based on Ray Kurzweil's book, co-directed and co-produced with Kurzweil. Screened at the Woodstock, Warsaw, Montreal World and Breckenridge film festivals.

Immersive theater · California Academy of Sciences · Consulting Producer

Kepler — Immersive Dome Theater

An immersive theater production integrating a live actor with fulldome projections, telling the story of 17th-century astronomer and mystic Johannes Kepler. Directed by Nina Wise, with music by cellist and composer Zoë Keating.

Fulldome experience · Producer

Seattle World's Fair 50th Anniversary Dome Experience

A 30-minute immersive dome journey traveling out from Earth to the edge of the known universe, exploring the fractal patterns that emerge at every scale — produced for the 50th anniversary of the Seattle World's Fair.

TED · Producer / Designer

Realtime VR for the TED Stage

Produced and designed early realtime VR demonstrations for the TED stage with Ray Kurzweil — bringing his digital avatar "Ramona" (performed by Pauley Perrette) to life.

Dance / multimedia · Contributing artist & technical consultant

"Okeanos" — Capacitor Dance Company

A performance combining inventive dance vocabulary, contemporary circus, abstract structures, sculptural costumes and poetically integrated audio-video multimedia, produced in partnership with ocean conservation organizations.

Realtime visuals · Live events

United Visual Artists — Custom D3 Software

Work with United Visual Artists' D3 realtime visual production software, spanning creative coding, data visualization, live events and VJ performance — a practice that began with the Noise Laboratories video arts collective, co-founded in Boston in 1996, and international VJ performance.
