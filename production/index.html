Immersive theater production at the California Academy of Sciences integrating live actor with dome projections telling the story of 17th Century astronomer and mystic Johannes Kepler. Directed by Nina Wise with music by famed cellist and composer Zoe Keating.

CLIENT: Motion Institute/California Academy of Sciences
