Toshi Anders Hoo

Art, work and musings.

Metaphors are the building blocks of the stories that make up our understanding of the world.

Toshi Anders Hoo is an award-winning media producer, designer and artist who specializes in assembling and managing interdisciplinary teams of artists and engineers. He works with expert teams of creatives and technologists to produce interactive and immersive exhibits, environments, presentations and live events that leverage emerging technologies — and provides strategic research and consulting to clients seeking a deeper understanding of emerging technology trends and the business opportunities and risks that come with them.

From 2016 to 2026 he directed the Emerging Media Lab at the Institute for the Future, and for more than a decade he advised futurist Ray Kurzweil on emerging media technologies. Today he co-runs Possibility Spaces (consulting and strategy) and pSpace Studio (generative and interactive art practice) with partner and collaborator Liza Bender.
