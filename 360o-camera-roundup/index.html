360º Camera Roundup

2015-11-17

My friend Jason Fletcher at the Full Dome Blog just posted a ridiculously comprehensive round up of all the 360º camera systems out (and even a few that almost launched).

I spent some time with the Ricoh Theta and Kodak PixPro this summer and both have already been updated with increased resolution and FOV.

It's amazing how quickly this field is developing at both the consumer and pro ends of the spectrum.

If you're into VR or dome film production be sure to explore the Full Dome Blog which covers, tools, content and the latest industry news.
